Try it✓ done

Pick the better cut, then see why.

A: 45 seconds showing the whole process vs B: 18 seconds showing the three best moments

B. Nobody needs the whole process. They need to believe you can do it. Three good moments does that faster and people watch to the end, which the algorithm notices.

A: keeping the bit where you fumble slightly and laugh vs B: cutting it out for a clean take

A, usually. A small genuine moment is the thing that makes a business feel like people rather than a brand. Cut the boring parts, not the human ones.

A: ending on the finished product vs B: ending with three seconds of the shop front and the opening hours

A. Ending on the good bit means people rewatch, which counts for you. Hours and address belong in the caption, where they are also readable and searchable.

No. A phone from the last five years, a window, and ten quiet minutes. Light, sound and distance make the difference, and all three are free.

No. You can run a whole account on videos of the place and the things you make, and never appear yourself.
