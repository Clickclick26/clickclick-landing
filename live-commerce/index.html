Live & social commerce

Live commerce software for quality, sales and scale

ClickClick builds the software that keeps live shopping consistent, optimises what it sells, and runs it across every platform from one place. Brands save time and money, and add more live output without adding people. Official TikTok and Amazon Partner, based in Belfast.

What live commerce is really about

Going live is the easy part. The value is in everything around it: keeping quality consistent show after show, understanding what actually drives sales, running the same catalogue across TikTok Shop, Amazon Live and your own channels, and doing all of that without the workload growing every time you add a platform or a creator.

That is what our software is built for. Live commerce, done properly, is quality management and sales optimisation across multiple platforms, designed to save time and money and to scale.

Quality management

Every show, clip and creator meets the same standard. Templates, checks and approvals are built into the workflow, so brand consistency does not depend on someone catching problems after the fact. The platforms are designed with relationship management and the human touch in mind, built for consistency, collaboration and long-term partnerships.

Sales optimisation

Live commerce is measured against revenue, not views. The software ties orders back to the stream, the product and the host, so you can see which formats, offers and creators perform and put more behind them. A live moment is also cut into shoppable clips that keep selling as on-demand content.

One workflow, every platform

As an official TikTok Partner and Amazon Partner, ClickClick runs live shopping across TikTok Shop, Amazon Live and the owned channels you control end to end. The catalogue, pricing and stock stay in sync everywhere, so your team is not rebuilding the same show three times.

Built to save time and money

  • More live output from the same headcount.
  • No duplicated setup across platforms.
  • Fewer review rounds, because quality checks are built in.
  • Shared reporting instead of manual reconciliation.
  • Creators onboarded without handing over the brand account.

Scalability in mind

The workflow is the same whether you run one show a month or one a day, on one platform or four. Adding a creator, a market or a channel does not mean rebuilding your process. ClickClick is a software company, not a production agency, and we stay involved in the strategy that keeps the programme growing.

Who it is for

Retail and consumer brands that already invest in video and want it to drive measurable sales, and the creators and hosts who present for them. ClickClick has worked with brands across the UK and Ireland, including L’Occitane, Revolución de Cuba and Astrid & Miyu, and is backed by Invest Northern Ireland.

Live commerce FAQ

What is live commerce software?

Live commerce software runs shoppable live video and manages everything around it: consistent quality across shows, a synced product catalogue, in-stream checkout, creator access, multi-platform publishing and sales reporting. ClickClick builds this software for brands and their creators across TikTok Shop, Amazon Live and owned channels.

How does live commerce software save time and money?

One workflow runs every platform, so a team is not rebuilding the same show for TikTok Shop, Amazon Live and its own site. Quality controls keep output consistent without extra review rounds, and shared reporting removes manual reconciliation. The result is more live output from the same headcount.

Do you support TikTok Shop and Amazon Live?

Yes. ClickClick is an official TikTok Partner and Amazon Partner, so our live commerce work spans TikTok Shop, Amazon Live and a brand’s own website and app from a single workflow.

Is ClickClick an agency or a software company?

ClickClick is a software company. We build the live commerce platform and workflows, and we provide the strategy to run them. We’re not a production agency and don’t sell video shoots.
