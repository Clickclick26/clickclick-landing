Live commerce software, built for brands that sell on video

What is live commerce?

Live commerce — also called live shopping — is selling products through live video. A host demonstrates items in real time and viewers buy without leaving the stream. It grew up on Chinese platforms, moved to TikTok Shop and Amazon Live, and is now a core channel for consumer brands in the UK and Ireland.

The hard part isn't going live. It's everything around it: keeping the product catalogue in sync, giving creators controlled access, capturing orders, and knowing afterwards which stream, clip or creator actually drove revenue. That's what our software does.

What ClickClick's live commerce software does

  • Live shopping streams with the product catalogue, pricing and stock tied to the broadcast, and add-to-basket or checkout in the stream.
  • Shoppable video and clips so a live moment keeps selling as on-demand content afterwards.
  • Creator and influencer workflows — brief, approve, schedule and give hosts the access they need without handing over the brand account.
  • Multi-channel publishing across TikTok Shop, Amazon Live and a brand's own website and app.
  • Sales and attribution tracking so live commerce is measured against revenue, not views.
