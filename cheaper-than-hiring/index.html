ClickClick Live for retailers

Live shopping costs less than hiring someone

ClickClick Live is software plus a trained host. For a small shop, that usually costs less than one extra staff member. The host can sell on live video in a way a new hire often cannot. See the software itself, or change the numbers below to your own.

Your numbers

£28,000 is a typical extra person in the UK before tax on top. £14,400 is an example year for software and a host. Type your real figures.

Why this is cheaper than a person

A new hire needs a wage, tax, holiday, and time to learn. They still may not be good on camera. ClickClick Live gives you the software to run the show, and a host who already passed the training. You are not paying for idle hours. You are paying for live sales.

What the host actually does

Before a show they run a 90-second check: sound, connection, light, frame, and the product list. During the show they sell, answer questions, and keep the stream on brand. After the show you keep the clip. That is more income potential than one extra pair of hands on the shop floor, for less than that person’s year.

Who this is for

  • Small retailers who sell products people can see on camera.
  • Shops that cannot justify a full-time content person.
  • Teams who want live on TikTok Shop, Amazon Live, or their own site without building a studio.

Tiny neighbourhood spots (cafes, salons, gyms) use CLocal instead. Same family. Smaller, closer, hyper local.

Questions

Is live shopping cheaper than hiring a staff member?

For most small retailers, yes. One extra person in the UK often costs about £28,000 a year before you add tax and pension. ClickClick Live is software plus a trained host. You pay for the shows, not a full-time wage, and the host is already trained to sell on live video.

Who is ClickClick Live for?

Small and mid-size retailers who want to sell on live video without hiring a full-time presenter or a video team. Cafes and hyper-local spots use CLocal. Shops that sell products on camera use ClickClick Live.

What do you get besides software?

A trained host. Creators complete ClickClick Academy and earn a live host mark. They already know the show flow, the checks before going live, and how to sell without sounding like an advert.

Is ClickClick an agency?

No. ClickClick is a software company in Belfast. We build the live shopping platform and train hosts. We do not sell video shoots.
