Questions people actually ask

Do I need followers?

No, and we never ask. A brand booking UGC is buying footage it can run as an ad, not access to your audience. What matters is what you can film, where you can film it, and how good it looks.

Do I have to buy the course to work with you?

No. If you are already working properly, send a few pieces to hello@clickclick.video and we will look at the work instead. The certification exists for people who want to be taught the whole job, and it is the fastest route onto the roster, but it is not a toll gate.

What is the roster, exactly?

The directory of everyone who has passed a graded ClickClick certification. Each person has a credential ID a brand can check. When a brand asks us for creators, that is the list we send names from. You do not pay separately to be on it.

So will this get me paid work?

It puts you in front of brands that have already signed off a budget, which is a far shorter queue than cold outreach into silence. But no certificate anywhere guarantees a booking, and we are not going to sit here and tell you otherwise. What you definitely walk away with is the craft, the five templates, the pieces you build as you go, and a credential a brand can check.

I have started courses before and never finished them. Will this be the same?

Possibly, and that is usually the course's fault rather than yours. Most of them are eleven hours of talking with nothing to do at the end. Every lesson in here finishes with one thing made, filmed or sent, which is short enough to get done in a gap and gives you something to show even if you stop halfway. There is no expiry breathing down your neck either: 12 months on the standard version, and no expiry at all on the other one.

Can I do it around a job, kids, or weeks where I have nothing left?

Yes, and it was built assuming you are. There are no live calls, no cohort, and nothing you have to log in for at a set time. Lessons are short on purpose. Disappear for a month and it will be exactly where you left it, with nothing marking you down for it.

How long does it take?

However long your life allows. Eight modules and 32 lessons, at your own pace, with no cohort date and nothing to turn up to.

Do I need a proper camera or a studio?

No. The whole thing is built around a phone, and a full module is about making a corner of wherever you already live look right for very little money. Brands buying UGC specifically do not want it looking like a studio.
