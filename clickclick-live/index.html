Nothing goes live on a guess

If something goes wrong mid-show, one control ends it everywhere at once. Nobody is scrambling through separate accounts while the wrong thing stays live.

Everyone in the show has a seat in the software

A show has roles: a host, standby cover in case someone drops out, a moderator watching the comments, and a director deciding what goes on screen next. Each of them gets their own screen and their own controls, and they can all be in different houses. You set who does what before the show starts, not while it is running.

What lag actually costs

Every extra second of buffering costs you viewers. Akamai’s analysis of 370 million real video plays found that a single rebuffering event pushes about 1% of viewers to leave on the spot, and as many as 40% leave after just one interruption over two seconds. Shows that stay under a 0.5% rebuffering ratio (the industry term for how much of the total watch time is spent stuck buffering, not actually playing) finish 90% of the time. Above 1%, that drops to 50%. That is why the pre-show check tests your connection before anyone is watching, not during the show.

What a no-show actually costs

UK employers lose several working days per employee a year to sickness and other absence on average, and unplanned time off costs a business real money in disruption, not just the missed wage. A host not turning up is the same problem, at the worst possible moment: live, in front of the people you wanted to impress. That is why standby cover is built in. If the main host does not show, standby already knows, instead of the audience finding out first.

What a studio actually costs

A professional studio in the UK typically runs somewhere between £30 and £170 an hour depending on the city and the kit, which adds up fast for a weekly show. A basic home setup (lighting, a mic, a phone or camera) usually costs less than a single day of studio hire, once, and then it is paid for. Every show on ClickClick Live runs from wherever the host already is: a kitchen, a shop floor, a living room. No studio booking, no travel, no day rate.
