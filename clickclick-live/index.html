What the software actually does

Live commerce (also called live shopping) is selling products through live video: a host shows the products, viewers ask questions in the comments, and they buy without leaving the stream. ClickClick Live is the software that runs that show, end to end.

Nothing goes live on a guess

Every platform, one screen

Why it also increases sales

These aren’t abstract features. Each one is there because of a real, measurable cost it stops you paying.

What lag actually costs

Akamai’s analysis of 370 million video plays found a single rebuffering event pushes about 1% of viewers to leave on the spot, and up to 40% leave after just one interruption. That is why the pre-show check tests your connection first, not mid-show.

What a no-show actually costs

UK employers lose several working days per employee a year to sickness and absence on average, and unplanned time off costs real money in disruption. That is why standby cover is built in, not bolted on.

What a studio is really for

A studio is hired by the hour, every time, and a single day of it costs more than setting up a corner of somebody’s own space once and being done with it. Every show runs from wherever the host already is, and the pre-show check holds it to the same standard a studio would, sound, light and frame, without hiring one or hiring a crew to run it.

Questions about the software

What is ClickClick Live?

ClickClick Live is the live shopping software that runs the actual show: a pre-show check, one shared comment feed across every platform, and a buy button that stays live wherever someone is watching.

How do I see it running?

Open the live demo linked on this page. It is the real player, not a video of one: the same product rail, buy-now flow and director controls used to run a real show.

Which platforms does the buy button work on?

The brand’s own website player, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, so a show is never tied to one platform.

What happens if a show goes wrong?

One stop control ends the show on every connected platform at once. Nobody hunts through five separate apps to shut things down.

What do I need to run a show?

A phone or a camera, your products, a decent internet connection, and someone willing to talk. The software handles the pre-show check, the comments and the buy button.

What does it cost?

It is a monthly licence, priced per brand, with plans that grow as your shows do. What it costs depends on how many shows you want a month, how many places you go live at once, and whether you also want one-to-one appointments on the same platform. Tell us what you sell and you will get a figure on the first call, not after three meetings.

Do I still need a production crew?

You still need a host, cover and somebody on comments. What you no longer need is a senior production crew standing by for every show: a director, a streaming engineer and a sound engineer. The pre-show check does that quality-control job instead, so a part-time team can run a show safely. The real saving is not in wages, it is that the job stops needing specialists and stops needing your evening.

What is live commerce in simple terms?

Live commerce is a live video broadcast where a host shows products and viewers can buy them straight away, without leaving the stream. It combines a live stream, a shoppable product list and checkout in one experience. It is also called live shopping.

Where does live commerce happen?

Mostly on TikTok Shop and Amazon Live today, and increasingly on brands’ own websites and apps. Instagram, YouTube and retail media networks also run live shopping formats. ClickClick Live runs your own website player alongside YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, all from one place.
