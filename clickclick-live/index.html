Nothing goes live on a guess

Every show starts with a 90-second check: sound, connection, light, frame and brand, and the show itself (products loaded, first line ready, who is backstage). Whatever would have gone wrong in front of an audience gets caught before it does, not during the show.

Every platform, one screen

Comments from your website, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok land in a single feed. Reply, pin, hide, block, or stop the whole show, without switching between five apps while a sale is happening.

A buy button nobody has to hunt for

Whoever is watching, on whatever platform, sees a working buy button pointed at exactly what is live right now. Tap it, buy it, without leaving the stream.

One control, not five logins

If something goes wrong mid-show, one control ends it everywhere at once. Nobody is scrambling through separate accounts while the wrong thing stays live.

A full crew, booked before the show starts

A main host (or a few, each from their own home), standby cover if someone drops, a moderator, and a director calling what is live. All of it booked ahead, not worked out on the day.

Questions about the software

What is ClickClick Live?

ClickClick Live is the live shopping software that runs the actual show: a pre-show check, one shared comment feed across every platform, and a buy button that stays live wherever someone is watching.

How do I see it running?
