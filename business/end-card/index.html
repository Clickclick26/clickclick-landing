End card maker

The last two seconds of a video should say who you are and where to find you. Type it in, pick your colour, and save it. Then drop it on the end of any video in whatever app you use.

Background colour

On an iPhone it goes straight to your Photos, so it is there when you open Instagram or your editing app.

1080 × 1920, the size for Reels, TikTok and Shorts

How to use it

  1. Save the card to your phone.
  2. Open your video in whatever app you edit in.
  3. Add the card as the very last clip, and set it to two seconds.
  4. Put your address and opening hours in the caption as well. People read the caption; they do not pause on the last frame.

Your words sit in the middle of the card on purpose. The bottom of the screen and the right-hand edge get covered by the app's own buttons, so anything there would be hidden.
