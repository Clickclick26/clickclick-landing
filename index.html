FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Straight answers on what ClickClick does and how live commerce works.

What does ClickClick do?

ClickClick is a video marketing software company based in Belfast. We build proprietary platforms for live commerce, social listening, influencer marketing, TV advertising and motion/video signage — and provide the strategy to run them — so brands can turn video content into sales.

What is live commerce?

Live commerce (also called live shopping) is selling products through live video, where viewers watch a host demonstrate items and buy them in real time without leaving the stream. ClickClick builds the platforms and workflows that make live commerce work for brands and their creators.

Does ClickClick work with brands outside Belfast and Northern Ireland?

Yes. ClickClick is based in Belfast City Centre and backed by Invest Northern Ireland, and we work with brands across the UK and Ireland — including L’Occitane, Revolución de Cuba and Astrid & Miyu.

Is ClickClick an agency or a software company?

ClickClick is a software company. We build the video marketing platforms brands use to run live shopping, social listening, influencer campaigns and multi-screen distribution, and we support the strategy behind them. We’re not a production agency and don’t sell video shoots.

What is social listening?

Social listening is monitoring conversations across social platforms to understand what audiences say about a brand, product or category. ClickClick’s social listening signals filter that noise down to the moments and trends worth acting on.
