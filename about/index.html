About

We build the software behind modern video marketing

ClickClick is a video marketing software company based in Belfast City Centre. We make proprietary platforms for live commerce, social listening, influencer marketing, TV advertising and motion/video signage, and we help brands run them.

Who we are

ClickClick was founded by experienced agency owners and IBM-trained, certified software engineers. Creative leadership and technical depth in the same room. That combination is the point: we have sat on the brand side, run the campaigns, and now build the tools we always wished existed.

We are a software company, not a production agency. We do not sell video shoots. We build the platforms brands use to make video perform, and we stay involved in the strategy that makes them work.

What we believe

Our platforms are designed with relationship management and the human touch in mind, built for consistency, collaboration and long-term partnerships. They strengthen those things while saving clients time and money.

They are built in-house rather than pieced together from generic AI add-ons or rebadged third-party tools, and shaped by people who know the industry and developed alongside strong partners in it, so quality is protected and improved where it counts.
